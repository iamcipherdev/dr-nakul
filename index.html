FAQ

Common questions.

How do I book a consultation?
Tap any WhatsApp button on this page — you'll be connected directly. Share your concern and photos for an initial review, then a consultation is scheduled.
Is hair transplant permanent?
Yes — transplanted follicles are taken from DHT-resistant donor areas and typically grow for life. Proper aftercare matters, and Dr. Somani provides a complete post-procedure plan.
I'm from outside Jaipur. Can I still consult?
Absolutely. Video consultations are available for outstation patients, and in-person visits can be planned around your procedure date.
How much does a procedure cost?
Cost depends on your specific case and is discussed transparently during consultation. There are no hidden charges — you'll know the full plan before deciding.
Will the results look natural?
That's the core philosophy here — enhancements that suit your features, never overdone. During consultation you'll discuss realistic outcomes openly.
